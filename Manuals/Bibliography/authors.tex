\chapter{CFAST Developers}

The Consolidated Model of Fire and Smoke Transport (CFAST) is the product of a collaborative effort led by
the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) with contributions from the UL Research Institutes' Fire Safety Research Institute. Its developers and contributors are listed below.
